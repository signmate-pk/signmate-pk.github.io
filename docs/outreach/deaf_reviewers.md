## Who we are looking for

- **Reviewers (2-3):** Deaf adults whose main language is ASL, ideally ASL teachers. They check each of the 250 signs:
  is the English word right for this sign, is the gloss right, and (later) is the reference recording a good example?
  About [N] hours in total, remote, at their own pace. Paid.
- **Testers for the live set (at least 5):** Deaf ASL signers who try SignMate on a laptop and a phone and let us keep
  the landmark points (no video unless they choose) as a frozen test set that is never trained on (master-v4 5.1).
  About [N] minutes each, remote. Paid.

## Where to ask

Pick organisations you or your teacher can reach, and check each one's own contact page before writing:
national and regional Deaf associations in the USA or Canada, university ASL and Deaf Studies programmes, ASL teacher
associations, and Deaf-led accessibility companies. A personal introduction (a teacher, an interpreter) works better
than a cold email. Do not post the message in groups whose rules forbid research requests.
