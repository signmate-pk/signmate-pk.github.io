# Live demo checklist (exhibitions, school visits, partner meetings)

Applies to any public demo of SignMate. The demo runs the live website, saved for offline use, with the
offline copy on a USB stick as a backup. The 30-second script is in README.md ("Presenting SignMate").

## Once, at home (on Wi-Fi)
1. Windows: Settings > Privacy & security > Camera: camera access on.
2. Open Chrome or Edge (use only that one) at https://signmate-pk.github.io/ and press **Turn on camera**.
3. More > **Save for offline use** until it says "Saved: works offline". Optional: **Install as an app**.
4. Wi-Fi off, browser closed and reopened: the page, camera and **Guided tour** still work.
5. Put the offline copy (`npm run build:shared`, or the v3.0.0 release zip) on a USB stick and test it.
6. Voices: press Speak in English and in اردو, online and offline. If the caption says there is no Urdu voice,
   SignMate reads Roman Urdu; plan for that.
7. Practise: on **Words**, tap a sign > **Practise this sign**, 10 tries each. Keep signs that show "That
   matches" at least 8 times out of 10 (start with Assalam-o-alaikum, hello, thank you, water, doctor, you).
8. Turn on More > Settings > Developer panel once and note the numbers for this laptop; turn it off again.

## Night before
- [ ] Windows Update paused; sleep "when plugged in": Never; Do not disturb on.
- [ ] Teams, Zoom and OneDrive sync closed.
- [ ] Site opened once with Wi-Fi off.
- [ ] Laptop and phone charged; charger, USB stick, phone stand, printed QR code (More > Share SignMate) packed.

## Morning of
- [ ] Site opens from the bookmark on **Words**; language set; camera tips (light, distance) pass at the stall.
- [ ] Demo signs tried once; the voice heard from 2 metres.
- [ ] Settings > **Presentation mode** on; **Guided tour** ready in a second tab as the backup.

## Never claim
- That signs are validated by Deaf signers: none are yet. Every sign is a suggestion the person confirms.
- Accuracy without its test: 98.6 % is on new recordings of the same dataset (weaker); on one new signer it
  was right first time on 5 of 14 signs.
- That the guided tour is live signing: it replays recorded signs (it says so on screen).
- That SignMate replaces an interpreter.
