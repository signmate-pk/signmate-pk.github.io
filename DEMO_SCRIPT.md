The **30-second** version is in [README.md](README.md#presenting-signmate-a-30-second-demo).

## 3-minute version
1. **Why (20 s).** "Pakistan has a large Deaf community and very few interpreters. Most everyday
   conversations, at a clinic counter or a shop, happen with pen and paper. SignMate puts a helper on the
   phone people already own."
2. **One word (30 s).** Words screen: sign **Assalam-o-alaikum**, lower your hands, show the confidence ring,
   tap **Yes, speak it**. Switch the language to اردو and tap a gallery sign > **Hear the word**.
3. **Practice (20 s).** Tap **Doctor** in the gallery: the real PSL dictionary video. **Practise this sign**,
   sign it: "That matches."
4. **A sentence (40 s).** Sentences screen: sign **Help**, then **Doctor**. Point at the pause ring, tap a word to
   show Remove / Swap, then **Speak**. If a sign comes up as "Which one did you sign?", show that as a feature:
   "When it isn't sure, it asks. It never speaks a guess."
5. **The reply (30 s).** Reply screen: the last sentence is on top. Tap "The doctor is coming." > **Full screen** >
   **Turn for the other person**. Play the PSL video.
6. **Under the hood (30 s).** Settings > Developer panel: frames per second, tracking and model time (a few ms
   per sign), WebGL or WASM. "Everything runs here; no video is sent anywhere." Mention How well it works:
   every number with its test, including the weak one (one new signer).
7. **Close (10 s).** "Next: testing with Deaf signers and growing to the signs people actually need."

If the camera or light fails at any point: More > **Guided tour**.
